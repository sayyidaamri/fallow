Fallow is a soil assessment for home gardens and small plots. We read fifteen markers from one core and set them beside readings from other gardens, so that people can learn how their ground has changed. Nothing in a Fallow report is a land survey, a contamination clearance, or a certificate of any kind. Where the law asks for a qualified surveyor, please use one.
