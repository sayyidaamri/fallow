Why Fallow?

What’s in the bed you dig every spring, and the lawn you just inherited? What about the compost you spread and the paths you wear? Your ground keeps records you can’t see.

By reading your soil layer by layer across a 30-season timeline, Fallow™ shows what changed, roughly when, and what might be worth doing about it, so next year’s beds start from something firmer than a hunch.

The difference

A scoop of soil is an average.
A Fallow™ core is a diary.

Nothing in a Fallow report is a land survey, a contamination clearance, or a certificate of any kind. Where the law asks for a qualified surveyor, please use one.
